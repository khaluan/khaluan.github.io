There is something useful about beginning with a small page and giving it room to grow.

This blog is a place for working notes, half-formed ideas, and the occasional finished thought. The first version does not need to be perfect. It only needs to be easy to return to.

> Make the next step visible, then take it.

For now, that means keeping a short list of things worth noticing:

- A question that keeps returning
- A tool that made the day easier
- A paragraph that deserves another look

The shape of the site will change as the writing does. That feels like the right place to start.
