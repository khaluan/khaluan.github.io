## Three small discoveries

This week was less about finishing a grand project and more about paying attention to the tiny decisions that keep a project moving.

### 1. Leave a trail

Short notes make it easier to pick up a task after a break. A useful note answers three questions:

1. What was I trying to do?
2. What did I learn?
3. What is the next observable step?

### 2. Prefer the visible draft

An imperfect page in a browser reveals more than a perfect plan in a notebook. Spacing, rhythm, and the awkward parts of a paragraph only become obvious once they have a place to live.

### 3. Keep the edges soft

Not every idea needs a category immediately. Some notes are allowed to remain between design, code, and ordinary life for a while.
