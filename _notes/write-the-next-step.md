---
title: End with the next step
topic: Writing
summary: The line that makes a note resumable
updated: 2021-04-25
---

The most valuable line in a working note is the last one: the next observable step. It is
also the first thing lost if the note is written after the fact rather than during.

Write it while the context is still in your head, phrased so that someone else could act
on it — including yourself in a month, who is effectively someone else.
