---
title: Keep a note to one idea
topic: Writing
summary: One note, one thing worth remembering
updated: 2021-04-22
---

A note that covers three things is really three notes, and none of them will be found
again. The test: can the title state the whole point?

If the title needs an "and", split it. The cost of a second file is nothing; the cost of
a note you cannot find is the whole note.
